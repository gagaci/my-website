If you see a person who can hardly make his bed, or someone with an endless drive to pursue the things they want, the difference is in dopamine. Most people think dopamine is the "happiness chemical." This is not the case — it is a chemical in our body that pushes us to seek resources: food, shelter, status, sex, and so on.

The most important thing to keep in mind is that dopamine is not infinite — it depletes over time. Imagine it as a container filled with water. Some activities take up more water and some take up little. After you initiate an activity, there is an increase in the dopamine system; after a period of time it decreases beneath the baseline.

Huberman explores this phenomenon amazingly. Imagine a person who lived 10,000 years ago. He wakes up and finds that there is minimal water and food. Dopamine forces him to seek out and find an animal to eat. As soon as he finds one, his level of dopamine drops to baseline — because, as we mentioned, dopamine is finite. Thus it reserves itself until the next hunt.

## Some tips to keep your energy [dopamine] level high

1. **Cold shower:** start with minimal exposure, and be careful.
2. **Do not stack dopaminergic activities:** going to the gym with pre-workout + coffee + music. This will diminish the value of the raw activity. Use extra energizers only when it is necessary.
